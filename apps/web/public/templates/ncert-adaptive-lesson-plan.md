## 2. Baseline Diagnostic Probe (Diagnose - 8 Mins)

_Goal: Identify foundational learning gaps before introducing abstract problem solving._

- **Prerequisite Check (Class 7 Review):**
  - Probe 1: Locating positive and negative integers on a horizontal number line.
  - Probe 2: Concept of equivalent fractions ($\frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{4}{8}$).
- **Diagnostic Tool:** BodhAI 3-probe adaptive quick-check.
- **Remediation Trigger:** If student accuracy < 66%, trigger Class 7 prerequisite micro-module.

---

## 3. Interactive Concept Exploration (Learn - 12 Mins)

_Goal: Concrete-to-abstract conceptual bridging via interactive number line visualizer._

- **Guiding Question:** How many rational numbers exist between $\frac{1}{4}$ and $\frac{1}{2}$?
- **Misconception Alert:** Address the common distractor assumption that there are "no whole numbers" between 1 and 2, hence no fractions between $\frac{1}{4}$ and $\frac{2}{4}$.
- **Multilingual Support:** Explain key terms in English and Hindi (परिमेय संख्या, समान हर).

---

## 4. Scaffolded Problem Solving (Solve - 15 Mins)

_Goal: Autonomous practice using 3-Tier Socratic Hint Ladders without spoon-feeding._

- **Adaptive Practice Problem:** Find three rational numbers between $\frac{1}{3}$ and $\frac{1}{2}$.
- **3-Tier Socratic Ladder Protocol:**
  - **Tier 1 (Metacognitive Nudge):** "What must be true about the denominators of two fractions before we can compare or split their interval?"
  - **Tier 2 (Conceptual Clue):** "Convert $\frac{1}{3}$ and $\frac{1}{2}$ into equivalent fractions with a common denominator of 12 or 24."
  - **Tier 3 (Step-by-Step Breakdown):** "$\frac{1}{3} = \frac{8}{24}$ and $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{12}{24}$. Now name any fractions with denominator 24 between 8 and 12."
- **Autonomous Rule:** Educator intervenes only if Tier 3 is reached without resolution.

---

## 5. Formative Exit Ticket & Adaptive Mastery Check (Test & Adapt - 10 Mins)

_Goal: Real-time Bayesian Knowledge Tracing update and next-step assignment._

- **Exit Ticket Problem:** Express a rational number between $-\frac{2}{5}$ and $-\frac{1}{5}$.
- **Mastery Criteria:** Bayesian Knowledge Tracing probability $P(\text{Mastery}) \ge 0.95$ across 3 consecutive unassisted attempts.
- **Teacher Heatmap Action:**
  - Group A ($P(\text{Mastery}) \ge 0.95$): Advance to real-world density word problems.
  - Group B ($P(\text{Mastery}) < 0.80$): Assign BodhAI offline-synced micro-practice cards.
